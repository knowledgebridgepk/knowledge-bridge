Free Guide

Documents you usually need for admission

Missing papers are the most common reason admissions are delayed. Keep these ready before you apply:

  • CNIC of parent / B-Form of student
  • Previous class result card or certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Worker or welfare registration proof
  • Fee challan or fee-support letter, if applicable

Requirements differ by institution. We confirm the exact list for you.

Tips before you apply

Apply earlySeats and welfare quotas fill quickly, so check deadlines first.
Check every form twiceSpelling mistakes in names or CNIC numbers cause rejections.
Keep copiesSave photocopies and phone photos of every document you submit.
Never pay unofficial "seat fees"Confirm all fees with the institution directly.

Your child's future should not wait on paperwork.

FAQ

Questions families ask us

Who can use Knowledge Bridge?

Workers, worker welfare beneficiaries, their children, and professionals such as HR managers who want to refer students.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will explain your options at no cost before you decide anything.

Which documents are usually needed?

Commonly a CNIC or B-Form, previous result cards, photographs and worker or welfare registration proof. We confirm the exact list for your case.

Can adult workers continue their own education?

Yes. Our worker self-education service helps with literacy, matric or intermediate through flexible study, and practical job skills.

How does the referral program work?

HR managers and professionals send us the student's details. We contact the family, guide the admission and keep the referrer updated.
